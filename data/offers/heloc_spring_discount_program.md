2. QUALIFYING CRITERIA & AUTO-DEBIT RELATIONSHIP DISCOUNT
================================================================================
To obtain and retain the 1.00% introductory APR discount, borrowers must satisfy the following terms:

A. Automatic Payment Enrollment
- The borrower must enroll in automatic electronic deduction (Auto-Debit / ACH) of the monthly payment from an eligible Meridian Trust & Savings Bank consumer checking account (such as Meridian Premier Checking or Meridian Choice Checking).
- Auto-debit setup must be finalized prior to or at loan closing.
- Loss of Discount Provision: If automatic debit payments are discontinued, cancelled, or terminated for any reason (including insufficient funds resulting in dishonored payments) during the 12-month promotional period, the 1.00% introductory APR discount will immediately cease. The APR will revert immediately to the standard variable rate without discount for the remainder of the loan term.

B. Credit Line & Collateral Guidelines
- Credit Line Range: Minimum credit line of $25,000; maximum credit line of $500,000.
- Property Qualifications: Eligible properties include 1-4 family owner-occupied primary residences and vacation single-family homes located within MTSB's licensed lending footprint. Manufactured homes, mobile homes, cooperatives, and commercial real estate properties are excluded.
- Credit Score & Underwriting: Minimum qualifying FICO® credit score of 700. Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio of 43%. Verification of employment, income, property hazard insurance, and satisfactory title insurance required.

3. INTEREST RATE STRUCTURE & INDEX DETAILS
================================================================================
- Variable Rate Benchmark: Meridian HELOC rates are variable and based on the highest Prime Rate published in the "Money Rates" section of The Wall Street Journal (WSJ Prime Rate) on the first business day of each calendar month, plus or minus a margin determined by credit score, line amount, and CLTV.
- Example Benchmark Calculation: Assuming a WSJ Prime Rate of 8.50%:
  * Standard Margin: +0.25%
  * Fully Indexed Rate: 8.75% APR
  * With Spring Auto-Debit Discount (-1.00%): 7.75% Introductory APR for Months 1-12
  * Month 13 and Subsequent: 8.75% variable APR (adjusting monthly with Prime Rate).
- Rate Caps & Floors:
  * Lifetime Interest Rate Cap: 18.00% maximum APR.
  * Lifetime Interest Rate Floor: 3.99% minimum APR.
  * The APR will never increase or decrease by more than 2.00% in any single calendar year after the introductory discount period ends.

4. DRAW PERIOD & REPAYMENT TERMS
================================================================================
- 10-Year Draw Period: During the initial 10-year draw window, borrowers may access funds via online banking transfer, special HELOC convenience checks, or Meridian Visa® HELOC access card. Minimum draw amount is $250.00. Minimum monthly payments during the draw period equal the accrued interest charges (interest-only) plus any billed fees.
- 20-Year Repayment Period: After the expiration of the 10-year draw period, the line closes to new advances and converts into a 20-year fully amortizing repayment period. Monthly payments during the repayment period will consist of principal and interest sufficient to fully amortize the outstanding balance over the remaining 240 months.
- Prepayment Penalty: There is NO penalty for paying down principal early or paying off the line in full ahead of schedule.
- Early Closure Fee: If the HELOC account is closed and terminated by the borrower within thirty-six (36) months of account opening, the borrower will be assessed an early closure fee of $500.00 to reimburse MTSB for waived third-party origination and closing costs.
