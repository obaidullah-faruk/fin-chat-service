2. DETAILED TERMS & BALANCE TRANSFER RULES
================================================================================
To benefit from the 0.00% introductory rate, the cardholder must adhere to the following stipulations:

A. 60-Day Promotional Transfer Window
- All balance transfer requests must be submitted, authorized, and initiated within sixty (60) calendar days from the original date of credit card account opening.
- Any balance transfer requested after the 60th calendar day will be processed at the standard non-promotional Balance Transfer APR (currently 17.99% to 25.99% variable, based on credit score) and will not be eligible for the 0% intro rate.

B. Balance Transfer Fee
- A transaction fee equal to 3% of the gross dollar amount of each transfer (with a minimum charge of $10.00 per individual transfer transaction) applies to all promotional transfers.
- The balance transfer fee is added directly to your outstanding credit card balance upon posting and will not accrue interest during the 0% intro period.

C. Allowable Transfer Balances & Limits
- Total balance transfers, including applicable transfer fees, cannot exceed the assigned available credit limit on your new Meridian credit card account.
- Meridian Trust & Savings Bank reserves the right to decline or partially fulfill transfer requests that exceed the available credit line.
- Transfers from Other Creditors Only: Balance transfers may only be executed from credit accounts issued by other financial institutions, retail department stores, or third-party card issuers. Transfers from existing Meridian Trust & Savings Bank credit cards, loans, lines of credit, or MTSB affiliate accounts are strictly prohibited.
- Processing Timeline: Balance transfers typically take between seven (7) and twenty-one (21) business days to complete and post to the payee institution. Cardholders must continue making scheduled minimum payments to existing creditors until the transfer reflects on their external account statement.

3. POST-PROMOTIONAL APR & REPAYMENT TERMS
================================================================================
- Standard Variable APR After Intro Period: At the conclusion of the 18 billing cycles, any remaining unpaid promotional balance will be assessed interest at the cardholder's standard variable Balance Transfer APR, which currently ranges between 17.99% and 25.99% variable APR, based upon the cardholder's credit profile established at account approval.
- Index and Margin: Standard APRs vary with the market based on the Prime Rate published in the Wall Street Journal (WSJ Prime Rate + margin of 9.49% to 17.49%).
- Minimum Monthly Payment: Cardholders must make at least the minimum monthly payment by the payment due date shown on each monthly billing statement (the greater of $35.00 or 1.5% of the total new statement balance plus billed fees and accrued interest).
- Forfeiture of Promotional Rate: Failure to make the required minimum monthly payment within sixty (60) calendar days of the payment due date will terminate the 0% promotional APR prematurely. If terminated, the remaining transferred balance will convert immediately to the Penalty APR of up to 29.99% variable APR.

================================================================================
4. ALLOCATION OF PAYMENTS & INTEREST ON PURCHASES (IMPORTANT WARNING)
================================================================================
- Grace Period on Purchases: If you transfer a balance and do not pay off your entire statement balance (including the transferred balance) in full each month, you will not receive a grace period on new purchases made on the card. Consequently, new purchases will immediately accrue interest at the standard Purchase APR (17.99% - 25.99% variable) from the transaction date until paid.
- Allocation of Excess Payments: In accordance with the Credit CARD Act of 2009, payments made in excess of the minimum monthly payment will be allocated by MTSB to balances with the highest APR first (typically new purchases) before being applied to lower-APR balances (such as the 0% promotional balance).
