SECTION 1: GENERAL TERMS AND ACCOUNT GOVERNANCE
================================================================================
This Deposit Account Agreement and Overdraft Privilege Terms ("Agreement") constitutes the legally binding contract between Meridian Trust & Savings Bank ("MTSB", "Bank", "we", "us") and each account holder ("Depositor", "Customer", "you", "your") establishing one or more consumer deposit accounts with the Bank.

By signing an Account Signature Card, establishing an account electronically, or maintaining an open deposit account with MTSB, you acknowledge receipt of and agree to all terms, disclosures, fee schedules, and funds availability policies set forth herein.

1.1 Account Ownership Classifications:
- Individual Accounts: Owned by one natural person. Upon death, funds pass in accordance with applicable probate law or designated beneficiary arrangements.
- Joint Accounts with Right of Survivorship: Owned by two or more persons. Each joint owner possesses full, independent authority to deposit, withdraw, transfer funds, or overdraw the account. Upon the death of one joint owner, title vests immediately in the surviving joint owner(s).
- Payable on Death (POD) / In Trust For (ITF): Designates named beneficiaries who receive funds only upon proof of death of all primary account owners.
- Fiduciary & Custodial Accounts: Accounts established under Uniform Transfers to Minors Act (UTMA), estates, conservatorships, or formal trust agreements.

SECTION 2: FUNDS AVAILABILITY POLICY (REGULATION CC, 12 CFR PART 229)
================================================================================
Our policy is to make funds from your cash and check deposits available to you on the first business day after the day we receive your deposit. Electronic direct deposits (ACH credits) and incoming wire transfers are available on the day of receipt.

2.1 Business Day Cut-Off Times:
Every day is a business day except Saturdays, Sundays, and federal holidays. Deposits received before 5:00 PM Central Time at an MTSB branch teller, or before 6:00 PM Central Time via Mobile Check Deposit or MTSB ATM, will be considered received on that business day.

2.2 Next Business Day Availability:
Funds from the following deposits are available on the first (1st) business day after the day of deposit:
- U.S. Treasury checks payable to you.
- Wire transfers.
- Checks drawn on Meridian Trust & Savings Bank ("On-Us" checks).
- State and local government checks, Cashier's checks, certified checks, and Federal Reserve Bank checks, if deposited in person to an MTSB employee.

2.3 Standard Check Availability:
- The first $225 of an aggregate deposit of checks on any one business day is available on the first (1st) business day following deposit.
- The remaining funds (up to $5,525) are available on the second (2nd) business day following the day of deposit.

2.4 Longer Delays (Exception Holds under 12 CFR § 229.13):
Funds deposited by check may be delayed for up to seven (7) business days under statutory exception conditions:
- Large Deposits: Aggregate check deposits exceeding $5,525 on any single business day (funds above $5,525 held up to 7 business days).
- Redeposited Items: Checks that have been returned unpaid by the paying bank.
- Repeatedly Overdrawn Accounts: Accounts overdrawn for at least six (6) business days during the preceding six months.
- Reasonable Cause to Doubt Collectibility: Notice of insolvency, stop payment notice, or verification issues with the paying bank.
- New Account Rule: For accounts open thirty (30) calendar days or less, standard availability schedules differ (full check deposits may be held up to 9 business days).

SECTION 3: BALANCE CALCULATIONS & TRANSACTION POSTING ORDER
================================================================================
Understanding how your balance is calculated and the order in which items post is critical to managing your account and avoiding overdraft fees.

3.1 Available Balance vs. Current (Ledger) Balance:
- Current (Ledger) Balance: The total balance of funds in your account at the close of business on the preceding day, plus all posted deposits, minus all posted debits. It does NOT reflect pending transactions.
- Available Balance: The portion of your account balance that is immediately accessible for withdrawal, check clearing, or debit card authorization. The Available Balance equals your Current Balance:
  * PLUS immediately available check deposits;
  * MINUS pending debit card authorizations placed by merchants (even if transaction settlement occurs days later);
  * MINUS administrative holds, deposited check exception holds, and pledged collateral holds.
OVERDRAFT ITEM DETERMINATIONS ARE CALCULATED EXCLUSIVELY AGAINST YOUR AVAILABLE BALANCE AT THE TIME AN ITEM IS PRESENTED FOR PAYMENT.

3.2 Daily Transaction Posting Order:
Transactions are grouped and processed at the end of each business day in the following established order:
1. Category 1: All deposits, incoming ACH credits, and interest credits are posted first to maximize available funds.
2. Category 2: Internal transfers, teller counter cash withdrawals, and incoming wire transfers (chronological order).
3. Category 3: ATM cash withdrawals and debit card transactions (posted in chronological order based on the timestamp assigned by the payment network).
4. Category 4: Recurring ACH debits and automated bill pay items (processed in order from lowest dollar amount to highest dollar amount to minimize total potential overdraft occurrences).
5. Category 5: Paper checks (processed in sequential check number order).

SECTION 4: OVERDRAFT PRIVILEGE SERVICE & REGULATION E OPT-IN
================================================================================
4.1 Overdraft Definitions:
- An overdraft occurs when you do not have enough Available Balance in your account to cover a transaction, but MTSB exercises its discretion to pay the item anyway.
- A Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) / Returned Item occurs when the Available Balance is insufficient and MTSB declines or returns the transaction unpaid.

4.2 Standard Overdraft Coverage vs. Extended / Affirmative Opt-In Coverage:
Under CFPB Regulation E (12 CFR § 1005.17), financial institutions cannot assess overdraft fees on everyday debit card transactions or ATM withdrawals unless the consumer affirmatively opts in.
- Standard Overdraft Coverage (Automatically Included):
  * Covers paper checks, automated recurring bill payments, and recurring ACH debits.
  * Everyday debit card transactions and ATM withdrawals will be automatically declined at no fee if funds are insufficient, unless you opt in to Extended Coverage.
- Extended Overdraft Coverage (Affirmative Opt-In Required):
  * By affirmatively electing Extended Coverage ("Opting In"), you authorize MTSB to consider paying ATM cash withdrawals and everyday one-time debit card purchases when your Available Balance is insufficient.
  * You may revoke your Opt-In election at any time without penalty via Online Banking, phone (1-800-555-6872), or at any MTSB branch.

4.3 Discretionary Overdraft Service:
Overdraft Privilege is a discretionary service, NOT an uncommitted credit facility or contractual loan. MTSB reserves the right to decline any transaction at any time for any reason, including account delinquency, excessive overdraft occurrences, or negative balance history.

SECTION 5: FEE SCHEDULE & CONSUMER PROTECTIONS
================================================================================
5.1 Transparent Overdraft Fee Schedule:
- Paid Overdraft Item Fee: $35.00 per item paid by MTSB into overdraft status.
- Returned Item (NSF) Fee: $0.00. (MTSB DOES NOT charge fees for returned checks or declined ACH debits).
- De Minimis Cushion: $0.00 fee assessed if your account is overdrawn by $15.00 or less at the end of the business day.
- Daily Fee Cap: Maximum of three (3) Paid Overdraft Item Fees per calendar day ($105.00 maximum daily overdraft fee cap). No fees are charged for fourth or subsequent items on that day.
- Continuous Overdraft / Sustained Negative Balance Fee: $0.00. (MTSB does not charge daily punitive fees for accounts remaining negative).

5.2 Overdraft Protection Linking Options (Fee-Free Alternatives):
To assist customers in avoiding overdraft fees, MTSB provides two linking alternatives:
1. Linked Savings or Secondary Account: Automatically transfers available funds from your linked MTSB savings, money market, or secondary checking account in exact increments of $50 to cover potential overdrafts. Fee: $0.00 per transfer.
2. Overdraft Line of Credit: Subject to credit underwriting and approval, an unsecured revolving line of credit ($500 to $5,000 credit limit) advances funds automatically. Interest accrues at the prime rate plus 6.99% APR, with NO per-transfer administrative fees.

SECTION 6: RIGHT OF SETOFF, NEGATIVE BALANCES & ACCOUNT CLOSURE
================================================================================
- Obligation to Repay: You agree to immediately deposit sufficient funds to bring a negative account balance back to positive within thirty (30) calendar days.
- Bank's Right of Setoff: By opening an account, you grant MTSB a security interest in and contractual right of setoff against all deposits and funds in any account held in your name (individually or jointly) to satisfy any matured debt or negative overdraft balance owed to the Bank, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable federal and state law (setoff excludes tax-qualified retirement plans [IRAs] and Social Security / SSI benefit deposits protected under 42 U.S.C. § 407).
- Account Termination: MTSB reserves the right to terminate any deposit account upon written notice to the account owner, or immediately without notice if fraudulent activity, criminal conduct, or abusive overdraft patterns are identified. Unclaimed positive balances are remitted via official check to the customer's last known mailing address.
