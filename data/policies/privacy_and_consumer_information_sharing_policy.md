SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & STATUTORY AUTHORITY
================================================================================
Meridian Trust & Savings Bank ("MTSB", "Bank", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and security of nonpublic personal information ("NPI") entrusted to us by our retail banking consumers and customers.

This Policy is formulated in strict accordance with Title V of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act of 1999 (GLBA, 15 U.S.C. § 6801 et seq.), the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's (CFPB) Regulation P (12 CFR Part 1016), Section 501(b) Interagency Guidelines Establishing Information Security Standards, and applicable state privacy statutes, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), California Financial Information Privacy Act (Cal. Fin. Code § 4050 et seq.), Vermont Consumer Protection Regulations, and Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A.

This Policy applies to all personally identifiable financial information collected or created in connection with consumer financial products or services provided by MTSB, including deposit accounts, consumer credit lines, residential mortgages, personal trust services, and digital banking platforms.

SECTION 2: TYPES OF INFORMATION MTSB COLLECTS
================================================================================
Under 12 CFR § 1016.3(n), "Nonpublic personal information" (NPI) encompasses any personally identifiable financial information provided by a consumer to obtain a financial product or service, resulting from any transaction involving a consumer financial product, or otherwise obtained in connection with providing financial services.

MTSB collects the following categories of NPI:
1. Identifying and Application Information:
   - Full legal name, residential address, telephone numbers, and email addresses.
   - Social Security Number (SSN), Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN), or foreign passport/visa numbers.
   - Date of birth, government-issued photo identification credentials (driver's license, passport, state ID).
   - Employment history, occupational title, annual gross income, and declared assets.

2. Transaction and Account Relationship Data:
   - MTSB account numbers, routing numbers, and linked external financial institution account details.
   - Ledger balance, available balance, daily account transaction histories, and electronic funds transfers (EFT).
   - Debit card numbers, card expiration dates, card security codes (CVV/CVC), and POS/ATM terminal records.
   - Payment histories, loan amortization schedules, collateral descriptions, and overdraft usage patterns.

3. Credit Bureau and Consumer Reporting Data:
   - Credit scores (FICO, VantageScore), trade line inquiries, debt service ratios, public records (bankruptcy, tax liens).
   - ChexSystems and Early Warning Services (EWS) account verification reports.

4. Digital Banking, Biometric, and Telemetric Data:
   - Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, device unique identifiers (UUID/IMEI), browser user-agent strings, geolocation coordinates.
   - Online and mobile session logs, biometric verification markers (facial recognition or fingerprint enrollment tokens stored in local device enclaves).

SECTION 3: HOW AND WHY MTSB SHARES CONSUMER INFORMATION
================================================================================
Financial institutions choose how they share personal information. Consumers have the legal right to limit some, but not all, sharing.

Category A: Permitted Sharing Without Opt-Out (Statutory Exceptions)
MTSB shares information under the exceptions described in 12 CFR §§ 1016.14 and 1016.15 for which consumer opt-out does not apply:
- Everyday Business Operations: Processing payments, clearing checks, administering deposit and loan accounts, posting Automated Clearing House (ACH) transfers, maintaining account records, and generating periodic statements.
- Risk Management and Fraud Prevention: Verifying customer identity, screening against OFAC SDN lists, detecting cyber intrusions, anti-money laundering monitoring, and reporting suspicious activity under the Bank Secrecy Act.
- Legal, Judicial, and Regulatory Mandates: Complying with lawfully issued subpoenas, court orders, IRS information filings (Forms 1099, 1098), examination demands by the FDIC, Federal Reserve Board, or CFPB, and state banking regulatory inquiries.
- Service Providers: Sharing with contracted third-party vendors performing core IT core processing (e.g., Fiserv/FIS), secure cloud hosting, statement rendering, and check imaging, subject to strict contractual non-disclosure and GLBA 501(b) security standards.

Category B: Sharing for Marketing and Joint Agreements (Opt-Out Eligible or Prohibited)
- MTSB Everyday Marketing: MTSB shares consumer data internally across bank departments to offer complementary banking, wealth advisory, and credit products. (Consumers cannot opt out of direct MTSB marketing regarding our own financial products, but may request placement on our Do-Not-Call/Do-Not-Email promotional suppression lists).
- Joint Marketing with Nonaffiliated Financial Partners (12 CFR § 1016.13): MTSB engages in contractual joint marketing arrangements with select credit card underwriters, insurance underwriters, and investment broker-dealers to offer specialized consumer financial products. Consumers have the right to opt out of this sharing.
- Nonaffiliates' Independent Marketing: MTSB DOES NOT sell, lease, or license consumer nonpublic personal information to nonaffiliated third parties for their own independent marketing or commercial exploitation.

SECTION 4: CONSUMER OPT-OUT CHOICES AND MECHANISMS
================================================================================
4.1 Scope of the Opt-Out Right
Pursuant to 12 CFR § 1016.10, consumer customers may exercise their statutory right to restrict MTSB from sharing NPI:
- Sharing with nonaffiliated third parties under joint marketing agreements.
- Sharing with affiliates regarding consumer creditworthiness or eligibility criteria under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA, 15 U.S.C. § 1681a(d)).
- Affiliates using consumer transaction and experience information to market their products or services (FCRA Affiliate Marketing Rule).

4.2 Opt-Out Exercise Channels
MTSB provides multiple accessible, free-of-charge opt-out channels:
1. Online Banking: Access Settings > Privacy Preferences in the MTSB Online Banking portal or mobile application.
2. Telephone: Call our dedicated Privacy Opt-Out toll-free line at 1-800-555-MTSB (1-800-555-6872), option 4, operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
3. Mail: Complete and mail the Opt-Out Election Form attached to the Annual Privacy Notice to:
   Meridian Trust & Savings Bank
   Attn: Privacy Compliance Operations, Mail Drop PCO-200
   100 Meridian Plaza, Suite 400
   Chicago, IL 60601

4.3 Joint Account Opt-Out Rule (12 CFR § 1016.9(g))
If two or more consumers jointly hold an account with MTSB (e.g., joint checking, co-borrowers), an opt-out direction submitted by any single joint account owner will be treated as applying to all joint owners on that specific account, unless the opting-out owner explicitly indicates that the election applies solely to themselves.

4.4 Processing Window and Duration
MTSB implements opt-out requests within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt. Once recorded, an opt-out selection remains effective indefinitely until revoked in writing by the consumer, or until the customer relationship is formally closed. Even after an account relationship terminates, MTSB continues to enforce the customer's recorded privacy preferences.

SECTION 5: STATE-SPECIFIC PRIVACY ADDENDA
================================================================================
5.1 California Residents (Cal. Fin. Code §§ 4050-4060 & CCPA/CPRA)
- Under the California Financial Information Privacy Act, MTSB will not share personal information with nonaffiliated financial companies for joint marketing unless the customer has provided affirmative written opt-in consent ("Important Privacy Choices for California Consumers" Form).
- California residents are automatically treated as having opted out of nonaffiliate sharing unless they submit an explicit opt-in election.
- CCPA/CPRA Notice: While financial data subject to GLBA is exempt from certain CCPA provisions, California residents retain the right to know what personal data is collected, request deletion of non-exempt personal data, correct inaccurate personal records, and be free from discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

5.2 Vermont Residents (8 V.S.A. § 10202)
- Vermont law mandates an opt-in standard. MTSB will automatically treat all customer accounts with a Vermont billing address as opted out of sharing with joint marketing partners and nonaffiliates, and will not share creditworthiness data among affiliates without explicit affirmative customer consent.

5.3 Nevada Residents (NRS Chapter 603A)
- In accordance with Nevada law, consumers may be placed on MTSB's internal Do-Not-Call registry by contacting our Privacy Operations unit at privacy@meridiantsb.com or calling 1-800-555-6872. Nevada residents may also contact the Bureau of Consumer Protection, Office of the Nevada Attorney General, 555 E. Washington St., Suite 3900, Las Vegas, NV 89101; Phone: (702) 486-3132.

SECTION 6: INFORMATION SAFEGUARDS & ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROLS
================================================================================
MTSB maintains a comprehensive written Information Security Program (WISP) compliant with Section 501(b) of the GLBA:
1. Technical Safeguards:
   - 256-bit AES encryption at rest for all database volumes, transaction repositories, and backup media.
   - TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all external network sessions, web portals, APIs, and mobile endpoints.
   - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for all internal administrative access and customer digital banking logins.
   - Real-time Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring, SIEM event correlation, and intrusion detection systems (IDS/IPS).
2. Physical Safeguards:
   - Biometric access badges, CCTV coverage, and mantrap turnstiles at all primary data processing centers and vault facilities.
   - Secure shredding and disposal protocols for paper records containing NPI in compliance with FACTA Rule (16 CFR Part 682).
3. Administrative & Personnel Safeguards:
   - Mandatory background checks, fingerprinting, and credit checks for all bank employees prior to onboarding.
   - Annual mandatory employee training on GLBA compliance, phishing defense, data classification, and social engineering counter-measures.
   - Strict role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring employees access consumer NPI strictly on a need-to-know basis.

SECTION 7: ANNUAL PRIVACY NOTICE DELIVERY & AMENDMENTS
================================================================================
MTSB provides the Privacy Notice upon initial account opening to each consumer.
Pursuant to the FAST Act amendment to GLBA (15 U.S.C. § 6803(f)) and CFPB Regulation P (12 CFR § 1016.9(c)(2)), MTSB utilizes the alternative online delivery method for annual privacy notices because:
1. MTSB does not share consumer NPI with nonaffiliates in any manner that triggers statutory opt-out rights beyond joint marketing exceptions;
2. MTSB has not altered its information sharing practices or policies since the previous notice; and
3. MTSB maintains the current, continuous privacy notice publicly accessible on our website at https://www.meridiantsb.com/privacy.

Consumers may request a printed paper copy of the Annual Privacy Notice at any time without charge by calling 1-800-555-6872 or visiting any MTSB retail banking branch.
