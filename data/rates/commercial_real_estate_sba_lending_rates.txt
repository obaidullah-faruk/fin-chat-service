1. INDEX BENCHMARKS & LENDING FRAMEWORK
================================================================================
Meridian Trust & Savings Bank provides comprehensive capital solutions for commercial enterprises, property investors, and small businesses. All commercial loans are subject to underwriting review by the MTSB Commercial Credit Committee.

Core Reference Benchmark Indices (as of November 1, 2026):
- Meridian Trust Bank Prime Rate: 7.75% (pegged to Wall Street Journal Prime Rate)
- 1-Month Term SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate): 4.85%
- 5-Year US Treasury Benchmark: 4.15%
- 10-Year US Treasury Benchmark: 4.30%

Underwriting Standards:
- Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): 1.25x for owner-occupied real estate; 1.30x for non-owner occupied investor commercial real estate.
- Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV): 70% to 80% depending on asset class and tenancy.
- Personal Guaranty: Required for all individuals owning 20% or greater equity interest in the borrowing entity.

3. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION (SBA) GUARANTEED LENDING
================================================================================
Meridian Trust & Savings Bank is designated as an SBA Preferred Lender (PLP), granting expedited in-house credit decisions without prior SBA agency review.

SBA 7(a) Working Capital, Equipment & Business Acquisition Loans:
Government-guaranteed term loans designed for creditworthy small businesses unable to obtain conventional credit under reasonable terms.
- Maximum Loan Amount: $5,000,000.00.
- SBA Guarantee: Up to 85% for loans <= $150,000; up to 75% for loans > $150,000.
- Permitted Spreads Over Wall Street Journal Prime Rate (Maximum allowable rates under SBA regulations):
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Loan Size Tier              Spread Formula            Current Floating Rate    Standard Maturity Term
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Up to $50,000.00            WSJ Prime + 4.25%         12.00% Variable          10 Years (25 Yrs for Real Estate)
$50,001 to $250,000         WSJ Prime + 3.25%         11.00% Variable          10 Years (25 Yrs for Real Estate)
$250,001 to $5,000,000      WSJ Prime + 2.75%         10.50% Variable          10 Years (25 Yrs for Real Estate)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SBA Express Revolving Lines of Credit:
- Maximum Line Amount: $500,000.00 (50% SBA Guarantee).
- Pricing Formula: WSJ Prime + 4.50% (12.25% variable rate).
- Turnaround Time: Initial credit decision within 36 business hours.

SBA 504 Real Estate & Heavy Machinery Fixed-Asset Financing:
Combines two loans with low borrower equity requirement (typically 10% down):
1. First Mortgage (MTSB Bank Share - 50% LTV):
   - 10-Year or 20-Year Conventional Fixed Term at Prime + 0.50% (currently 8.25%).
2. Second Mortgage (Certified Development Company / SBA Debenture - 40% LTV):
   - Fully amortizing 20-year or 25-year fixed debenture backed by 10-Year US Treasury + 1.85% spread (indicative effective borrowing rate ~6.65% including all servicing and CDC fees).
3. Borrower Equity: 10% minimum (15% for special-use properties or new businesses).

================================================================================
4. COMMERCIAL LINES OF CREDIT (WORKING CAPITAL)
================================================================================
Revolving credit facilities structured to support inventory financing, accounts receivable cycles, and seasonal working capital requirements.
- Commitment Sizes: $100,000.00 to $10,000,000.00.
- Variable Pricing Formula: WSJ Prime + 1.00% to Prime + 3.00% floating based on borrower liquidity and leverage.
- Borrowing Base Formula: Up to 80% of eligible accounts receivable (< 90 days) plus up to 50% of finished goods inventory.
- Annual Facility Review Fee: 0.25% of committed line limit.
- Unused Line Fee: 0.15% per annum on average unused portion, billed quarterly.

================================================================================
5. FEES, COVENANTS & CLOSING EXPENSES
================================================================================
- Commercial Loan Origination Fee: 0.75% to 1.25% of committed facility amount.
- SBA Guaranty Fee: Statutory fee established by SBA regulations (waived for veterans under SBA Veterans Advantage).
- Environmental Review Fee: Phase I Environmental Site Assessment ($2,500 - $3,500) where required.
- Commercial Property Appraisal Fee: Independent licensed MAI commercial appraisal ($3,000 - $6,500 depending on complexity).
- Standard Reporting Covenants: Submission of annual audited or reviewed financial statements within 120 days of fiscal year-end, and interim quarterly balance sheet and P&L statements within 45 days of quarter-end.
