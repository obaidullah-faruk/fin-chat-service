1. PRICING BASIS & GENERAL UNDERWRITING CRITERIA
================================================================================
Rates and APRs published herein represent Meridian Trust & Savings Bank standard retail mortgage pricing for owner-occupied, single-family primary residences. Quotes assume a purchase transaction or rate-and-term refinance with a representative credit score of 740+, an 80% Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio, and a standard 45-day rate lock period.

All mortgage loans are subject to full underwriting approval, verifiable income documentation, asset verification, clear title, and acceptable appraisal. Actual rates may vary based upon credit score, debt-to-income (DTI) ratio, property type, occupancy status, and loan amount.

Conforming Loan Limit: $766,550.00 for single-unit properties in standard counties ($1,149,825.00 in designated high-cost statutory areas).
Jumbo Loans: Mortgage loan balances exceeding conforming statutory limits up to $3,000,000.00.

**Adjustable Rate Mortgage (ARM) Details:

- Rate Adjustment Caps: 2/2/5 structure (Initial cap: max 2.0% adjustment at first change; Periodic cap: max 2.0% annual adjustment; Lifetime cap: max 5.0% above initial note rate). Minimum floor rate equals margin (2.750%).

4. REFINANCING OPTIONS: RATE-AND-TERM VS. CASH-OUT
================================================================================
1. Rate-and-Term Refinance:
   - Designed to lower monthly payments, shorten term duration, or convert an adjustable-rate mortgage to a predictable fixed rate.
   - Pricing: Identical to the purchase rate tables above.
   - Maximum LTV: 80% without Private Mortgage Insurance (up to 95% with standard PMI).

2. Cash-Out Refinance:
   - Enables homeowners to leverage home equity for debt consolidation, home renovation, or investment purposes.
   - Pricing Adjustment: +0.375% added to the base interest rate, or 0.750 points loan-level price adjustment (LLPA).
   - Maximum LTV: 75% for single-family primary residences (70% for 2-4 unit properties).
   - Maximum Cash-Out Proceeds: $350,000 on conforming loans ($750,000 on jumbo loans).

================================================================================
5. RATE LOCK POLICIES & FLOAT-DOWN AGREEMENT
================================================================================
- 30-Day Rate Lock: Complimentary upon full loan application submission and initial document verification.
- 45-Day Rate Lock: Standard default lock period with no fee.
- 60-Day Rate Lock: Available for a 0.125% fee (credited back to borrower at closing).
- 90-Day New Construction Lock: 0.25% upfront fee, refundable at closing.
- MTSB Float-Down Feature: If prevailing market interest rates on the locked product drop by 0.25% or more at least 10 business days prior to closing, the borrower may execute a one-time float-down option to the current market rate for an administrative fee of $250.00.

================================================================================
6. CLOSING COSTS, ESCROWS & SERVICING FEES
================================================================================
- Lender Processing & Underwriting Fee: $1,295.00 flat fee.
- Appraisal Fee: $550.00 - $750.00 (standard single-family; jumbo or complex properties higher).
- Tax Service & Flood Certification Fee: $85.00.
- Escrow Account: Required on all loans exceeding 80% LTV for real estate taxes and homeowner's insurance. Optional for conventional loans under 80% LTV (escrow waiver fee: 0.125% of loan amount).
- Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI): Required on conventional loans with LTV exceeding 80%. Automatically cancelled at 78% LTV per the Homeowners Protection Act of 1998.
