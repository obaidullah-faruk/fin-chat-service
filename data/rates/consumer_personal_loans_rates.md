================================================================================
1. REGULATORY DISCLOSURES & CREDIT TIERS
================================================================================
All loans are subject to credit qualification, income verification, and Meridian Trust & Savings Bank underwriting guidelines. Annual Percentage Rates (APR) are fixed for installment personal loans and auto loans unless explicitly noted.

MTSB Consumer Credit Underwriting Tiers:
- Tier 1 (Super Preferred): FICO Score 760 or above
- Tier 2 (Preferred):       FICO Score 700 to 759
- Tier 3 (Standard):        FICO Score 660 to 699
- Tier 4 (Non-Conforming):  FICO Score 620 to 659
Applicants with credit scores below 620 require an approved co-signer or pledged collateral under the MTSB Secured Credit Program.

Relationship Discount:
Borrowers who set up automatic recurring monthly payments (Auto-Pay) debited directly from an active Meridian Trust checking or savings account receive a 0.25% APR rate discount across all personal, debt consolidation, and auto loan products. Displayed rates below reflect the net rate WITH the 0.25% Auto-Pay discount applied.

================================================================================
5. LOAN SERVICING & ANCILLARY FEES
================================================================================
- Late Payment Fee: $25.00 or 5.0% of the overdue monthly payment, whichever is less, assessed after a 15-calendar-day grace period.
- Returned Payment / NSF Fee: $0.00.
- Loan Document & Origination Fee: $0.00.
- Title Processing Fee (Auto Loans): Pass-through fee equal to actual state DMV electronic lien titling fee (varies by state, typically $15.00 - $35.00).
