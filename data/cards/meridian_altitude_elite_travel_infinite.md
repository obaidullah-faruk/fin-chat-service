3. EXCLUSIVE TRAVEL PRIVILEGES & ANNUAL CREDITS
- $300 Annual Travel Statement Credit: Automatically applies to the first $300 in cumulative travel charges (airline tickets, seat upgrades, hotel stays, cruises, car rentals, passenger rail, and toll roads) incurred each cardmember year.
- Priority Pass Select Membership: Primary cardholders receive complimentary enrollment providing unlimited visits to over 1,400 airport lounges and luxury travel experiences worldwide. Includes complimentary entry for up to two (2) accompanying travel companions per visit.
- Global Entry / TSA PreCheck / NEXUS Credit: Cardholders receive a statement credit of up to $100 every four (4) years when charging the application fee to their card.
- Visa Infinite Luxury Hotel & Resort Collection: VIP status at over 1,000 premium worldwide properties, featuring complimentary room upgrades upon availability, daily continental breakfast for two, $100 food/beverage or spa amenity credit, guaranteed 3:00 PM late check-out upon request, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

4. REWARDS CURRENCY & ACCELERATOR TIERS
Meridian Altitude Points accrue on all qualifying card expenditures:
- 5x Points: On commercial flights, prepaid hotel reservations, and luxury resort stays booked directly through the Meridian Travel Experience online concierge portal.
- 3x Points: On all direct global airline, passenger train, rideshare, car rental purchases, and dining at verified restaurants, bistros, and international eateries.
- 1x Points: On all everyday general retail purchases.
- Points Transfer & Redemption: Points can be transferred 1:1 to participating airline and hotel loyalty partners, redeemed for statement credits at 1.00 cent per point, or booked through Meridian Travel with a 25% redemption bonus (10,000 points = $125 travel value).

5. COMPREHENSIVE TRAVEL & PURCHASE INSURANCES
- Primary Auto Rental Collision Damage Waiver (CDW): Provides primary reimbursement coverage up to $75,000 for physical damage or theft of rental vehicles worldwide when the entire rental transaction is billed to the card and the rental company's collision waiver is declined.
- Trip Cancellation & Interruption Protection: Up to $10,000 per covered traveler ($20,000 maximum per occurrence) for non-refundable prepaid travel expenses canceled due to covered illness, severe weather, or unforeseen emergencies.
- Trip Delay Reimbursement: Up to $500 per ticket for lodging, food, and personal essentials when a common carrier flight is delayed by more than six (6) hours or requires an unexpected overnight stay.
- Lost Luggage Reimbursement: Coverage up to $3,000 per covered passenger ($2,000 per bag maximum for checked items; $500 jewelry/electronics limit).
- Cell Phone Protection: Up to $1,000 per claim ($50 deductible; maximum 2 claims per consecutive 12 months) against theft or accidental physical damage when paying your recurring monthly cellular bill with the Altitude Elite card.
