1. COMMERCIAL FACILITY OVERVIEW
The Meridian Business Platinum Card is issued by Meridian Trust & Savings Bank to corporations, limited liability companies, partnerships, and sole proprietorships organized under U.S. law. Designed to optimize commercial working capital, streamline vendor payables, and provide centralized accounting transparency, this credit facility provides revolving limits from $10,000 to $100,000 based on underwriting review.

Maximize enterprise cash flow with rewards tailored to corporate overhead:
- 4% Cash Back - Cloud, SaaS & Telecom: Earn 4% cash back on cloud server hosting (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform), subscription software licenses, telecommunications, and high-speed enterprise internet services (up to $25,000 in annual category spend; 1.5% thereafter).

4. ADVANCED EMPLOYEE CARD CONTROLS & SPEND MONITORING
The Meridian Business Card Manager portal empowers primary account administrators with enterprise-grade operational controls:
1. Dynamic Sub-Limits: Establish unique monthly, weekly, or single-transaction spending caps on any individual employee card.
2. Merchant Category Code (MCC) Restrictions: Toggle permissions by industry code, automatically blocking prohibited categories such as casinos, adult entertainment, liquor retailers, or retail jewelry stores.
3. Geo-Fencing Controls: Restrict employee card charging privileges to specific domestic zip codes or approved international travel corridors.
4. Instant Card Freezing: Administrators can suspend or re-activate individual employee cards instantly via the mobile application.
5. Automated Receipt Matching & ERP Sync: Employees photograph receipts via mobile app; transactions automatically match and export directly into QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage Intacct, or SAP.
